QUESTIONS

FAQ

Is the APK safe to install?

Yes. It is built in public by GitHub Actions from this repository's source, then uploaded to GitHub Releases. Every release lists its SHA-256 checksum so you can verify the file. Android will warn about any app installed outside the Play Store — that is normal for direct APKs.

Why is it not on the Play Store?

Play Store publishing costs a one-time $25 developer fee and a review process. ZeeFlix is a free student project, so it ships as a direct APK plus a web app. You get updates faster this way.

Does it cost anything or show ads?

No. No ads, no subscriptions, no accounts, no analytics. ZeeFlix plays open-licensed films (Creative Commons) and only streams media you add and are allowed to use.

Which films are included?

Twelve titles from the Blender Foundation and Blender Studio open-movie collection, including Sintel, Tears of Steel, Big Buck Bunny, Spring, Cosmos Laundromat, Charge and Sprite Fright. Full credits are on the credits page.

Will there be an iPhone app?

Not yet — but the web app works in Safari and can be added to your home screen. An iOS build is on the roadmap.

Can I use my own movies?

Yes, in Providers. Add a direct video URL, an HLS/DASH manifest or an official embed you have permission to use. ZeeFlix does not host, scrape or decrypt anything.

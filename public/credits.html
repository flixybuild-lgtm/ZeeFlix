Rights & attributions

ZeeFlix is an independent student project by Zeeshan Haider. It is not affiliated with the filmmakers or registrars listed here. The discovery shelves are editorial selections, not measured audience charts.

The open cinema collection

  • Sintel (2010), directed by Colin Levy. © Blender Foundation, durian.blender.org. CC BY 3.0. The complete film is provided as a bandwidth-saving 640 × 272 WebM transcode, with its credits retained. Source distribution: MDN DOM examples.
  • Tears of Steel (2012), directed by Ian Hubert. © Blender Foundation, mango.blender.org, CC BY 3.0. English subtitles for Tears of Steel and Sintel are provided in WebVTT format, converted from the open film subtitles.
  • Big Buck Bunny (2008), © Blender Foundation, peach.blender.org, CC BY 3.0. The high-frame-rate edition credits the original team and the BBB3D rendering project.
  • Cosmos Laundromat: First Cycle, Spring, Charge, Agent 327: Operation Barbershop, Sprite Fright, and Caminandes: © Blender Foundation / Blender Institute / Blender Studio and the artists credited in each release. Agent 327 is based on Martin Lodewijk’s comics.
  • The Making of Sintel, documentary by Ali Boubred, distributed by Blender. No download offered by ZeeFlix.

Remote films are played from the publisher’s public media index or official embed. Each title links to its original release. Download availability is the intersection of the title’s authorization and the provider’s current download flag. Downloading is not a grant of new distribution rights; the original release’s license applies.

Artwork and typography

The seven-colour prism mark, interface and illustrative film key art were created for ZeeFlix. The poster illustrations are AI-generated interpretations, not official posters or frames from the films. Manrope, Anton and Bebas Neue are distributed under the SIL Open Font License. Manrope license · Anton license · Bebas Neue license.

Playback software

Rust / WebAssembly with native HTML5 playback. Optional adaptive streams use hls.js or dash.js only when needed. No DRM circumvention, scraping, proxying or paid subscriptions.
